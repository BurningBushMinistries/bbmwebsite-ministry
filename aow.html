Ministry Overview

Empowering women to thrive in every area of life

Army of Women creates space for women to be strengthened, equipped, and encouraged through Biblical truth, personal development, and community-centered support.

Vision

Setting women free from every bondage.

Mission

Equipping women physically, spiritually and economically.

Purpose in Action

Building women of faith, courage, and practical impact

This ministry is designed to support women holistically — helping them grow in the Word of God, serve their communities, strengthen intergenerational relationships, and gain skills that uplift lives.

  • Rooted in Biblical guidance and spiritual freedom
  • Focused on practical empowerment and community outreach
  • Committed to support, care, and leadership development
Strategic Goals

Five goals guiding the ministry vision

These goals shape how Army of Women serves women, families, and communities with intention and purpose.

Seek answers from the Word of God

Grounding women in truth, wisdom, and spiritual clarity through Scripture.

Reach out to communities

Extending care, compassion, and practical ministry beyond church walls.

Bridge the generation gap

Creating connection, mentorship, and unity between younger and older women.

Transfer skills

Sharing practical knowledge that empowers women in daily life, ministry, and work.

Establish support groups

Building spaces where women can find encouragement, healing, and community support.

Ministry Commissions

Six focus areas aligned to the goals

These commissions represent practical channels through which the ministry responds to real needs in society.

Workplace & Financial Wellness

Encouraging women to thrive in the workplace and steward finances with wisdom and confidence.

Community Development

Supporting upliftment initiatives that bring practical help and hope to communities.

Schools

Investing in the next generation through support, mentorship, and positive influence in learning spaces.

Gender-Based Violence

Creating awareness, support, and safe pathways for women facing abuse and injustice.

Health / Home-Based Care

Responding to physical and emotional needs through care, compassion, and support networks.

Social Media

Using digital platforms to inform, connect, encourage, and expand the ministry’s reach.
