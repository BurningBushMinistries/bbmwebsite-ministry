Ministry Overview

Equipping men to live with integrity, meaning, and purpose

Covenant Keepers is focused on shaping men spiritually, personally, and practically so they can lead well, serve faithfully, and make a meaningful impact in their homes, church, and society.

Vision

“Develop the whole man.”
2 Timothy 3:17

Mission

Develop soul, body and spirit while pursuing spiritual excellence in every sphere of life.

Purpose in Action

Forming men who stand firm in covenant, character, and responsibility

Covenant Keepers exists to strengthen men in faith and daily life, helping them become protectors, providers, leaders, and nation builders rooted in the truth of God’s Word.

  • Committed to spiritual excellence and practical maturity
  • Focused on responsibility in family, church, and community
  • Building men of integrity who understand covenant with God
Strategic Objectives

Four objectives guiding the ministry mission

These priorities shape how Covenant Keepers develops men with strength, discipline, and purpose.

Responsible Fathers

Advocating for responsible and proud fathers who lead their homes with care and wisdom.

Positive Habits

Inculcating positive habits and attitudes that shape godly character and discipline.

Voice of Integrity

Enabling the voice of integrity to be heard in every community and sphere of influence.

Skills Development

Encouraging practical growth and skill-building that benefits families, ministry, and society.

Background

The heart behind Covenant Keepers

A ministry birthed from prayer and the desire to build men according to God’s design.

Covenant Keepers is the men’s department within Burning Bush Ministries. It was birthed out of God’s prompting to the leadership of BBM that there is a need to focus intentionally on building up men within the church and, in turn, society.

The name “Covenant Keeper” emerged through prayer and seeking the Lord’s guidance. It reflects the kind of man God is seeking: a man who understands that he is in covenant first with God, and whose life is built on the truth and finished work of the Blood of Jesus Christ.

A covenant keeper understands that this covenant is not to be broken, but to be kept and held onto, so that he may live a life of meaning, purpose, and spiritual integrity.

The department encompasses all men — young and old, married and single. While different age groups may have different needs, no discrimination based on physical features is allowed.

A covenant keeper is a man who lives knowing that his life, choices, and purpose are anchored in his covenant with God.
The Pledge

What Covenant Keepers stand for

If the promotion of moral values is central to the organisation, the pledge reflects the convictions men are called to carry.

Protectors of Home

To be protectors and defenders of their homes with courage, stability, and responsibility.

Loving Fathers

To cherish and love their children with consistency, care, and godly example.

Providers

To provide for their families and serve as dependable pillars in their homes.

Law and Order

To uphold law and order in society and represent righteousness in public life.

Nation Builders

To be nation builders whose lives and leadership contribute to stronger communities and a better future.
