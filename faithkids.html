Faith Kids

Faith Kids is the children’s department of Burning Bush Ministries, focused on helping children know the Lord, grow in faith, and boldly share the Gospel from a young age.

Ministry Overview

Helping children know God and grow boldly in faith

Faith Kids is designed to make children’s ministry engaging, nurturing, and spiritually formative, helping children understand God’s love and live it out with confidence.

Vision

Making the Lord known throughout the generations to come.

Mission

Teaching children the fear of the Lord and empowering them to share the Gospel with boldness.

Purpose in Action

Creating a joyful space where children can learn, grow, and belong

Faith Kids focuses on building strong spiritual foundations in children through teaching, care, encouragement, and child-friendly ministry experiences that make God’s truth accessible and memorable.

  • Teaching children the greatness of God in age-appropriate ways
  • Creating child-friendly ministry spaces that are engaging and safe
  • Equipping leaders and families to support the faith journey of children
Strategic Goals

Key goals shaping the Faith Kids ministry

These goals guide how the ministry helps children encounter Jesus, grow in truth, and thrive in a supportive environment.

Acknowledge God’s Greatness

Helping children understand the greatness of God and the importance of accepting Jesus Christ.

Equip Children’s Ministers

Recruiting and equipping leaders who can serve children faithfully and effectively.

Child-Friendly Ministry

Making children’s ministry welcoming, engaging, and relevant to the needs of young learners.

Financial Stability

Ensuring stability and sustainability for the ongoing growth of the Faith Kids ministry.
